Executive Summary

An Employer of Record in Romania is a structured arrangement in which a licensed local entity becomes the formal legal employer of a worker who performs services for a client business, while the client retains day-to-day direction of the work itself. The function exists because engaging staff directly in Romania normally requires a registered employer, a payroll infrastructure and ongoing compliance with Romanian labour, tax and social security rules.

Operationally, the Employer of Record issues the Romanian employment contract, registers it in the REGES-ONLINE general employee register, obtains or applies a Romanian tax identification code with ANAF, calculates and withholds income tax and social contributions, and files the consolidated monthly declaration Form D112.

The Romanian legal framework for this function is anchored in the Labour Code (Law no. 53/2003), together with the annually adjusted national gross minimum wage, which stands at RON 4,325 per month for standard full-time work from 1 July 2026, up from RON 4,050 in the first half of the year.

Cross-border relevance is substantial because many Employer of Record clients are foreign companies without a Romanian legal entity. These businesses rely on the Employer of Record to lawfully employ staff in Romania, coordinate EU social security rules such as A1 certificates, and, where relevant, support work permit sponsorship for non-EU nationals through the General Inspectorate for Immigration.

Purpose

The purpose of the Employer of Record function is to allow a business to lawfully engage workers in Romania without first establishing its own Romanian legal entity, while ensuring that payroll, tax withholding, social contributions and labour register obligations are met correctly from the outset.

It exists to convert a hiring intention into a compliant Romanian employment relationship, reducing the administrative burden and misclassification risk that would otherwise fall on a foreign business unfamiliar with Romanian payroll and labour law.

Primary Outcome

A compliant Romanian employment relationship in which the worker holds a valid local employment contract registered in REGES-ONLINE, payroll and statutory contributions are administered correctly, minimum wage and Labour Code obligations are respected, and the client business retains operational direction of the work without carrying local employer-of-record liability.

Country Characteristics

Country characteristics explain the jurisdiction-specific features that shape how Employer of Record services operate in Romania. The section matters because Romanian employment practice is influenced not only by statute, but also by a strongly document-driven, filing-based administrative culture centred on ANAF and the Labour Inspectorate.

Operational Culture Romanian employment practice is statute-driven, with detailed procedural filing requirements and close administrative oversight through electronic labour and tax registers.
Legal Framework Orientation Statutory employment protection under the Labour Code operates alongside a nationally set minimum wage, meaning collective bargaining plays a smaller role in setting pay floors than in some other EU states.
Commercial Context A digitally evolving payroll and tax administration, mandatory REGES-ONLINE contract registration and defined statutory contribution rates make correct classification and timely filings commercially important from day one.
Language Expectation Romanian is required for statutory contracts and official filings, while English is frequently used in international business, group HR policy and cross-border payroll coordination.
Key Authorities

Key authorities identify the institutions that shape, administer or influence Employer of Record activity in Romania. Romanian employment compliance operates through an interaction between fiscal administration, labour inspection, social insurance administration and, where relevant, immigration control rather than through a single unified employment authority.

Official Name Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală (ANAF)
Official English Name National Agency for Fiscal Administration
Primary Role Central authority for employer tax registration, income tax withholding oversight, and collection of the consolidated monthly declaration covering income tax and social contributions.
Responsibilities Administers employer registration and the Romanian Tax Code, receives the consolidated monthly declaration Form D112, and enforces payment deadlines for withheld tax and contributions.
Typical Interaction The Employer of Record registers with ANAF, files Form D112 by the 25th of the following month and remits withheld tax and contributions on behalf of each employee.
Official Website anaf.ro
Cross-Border Relevance Central for foreign employers without a Romanian permanent establishment, who must still obtain a fiscal registration to process local payroll.
Official Name Inspecția Muncii
Official English Name Labour Inspectorate
Primary Role National authority overseeing labour relations compliance and administering the REGES-ONLINE general employee register, which replaced the former REVISAL system in 2025.
Responsibilities Requires registration of every individual employment contract in REGES-ONLINE, generally no later than one day before the employee starts work, and supervises workplace and contractual compliance.
Typical Interaction The Employer of Record registers each new contract, amendment, suspension or termination in REGES-ONLINE and maintains up-to-date employee records.
Official Website reges.inspectiamuncii.ro
Cross-Border Relevance Relevant whenever a foreign client directs day-to-day work performed physically in Romania, since labour register obligations attach to the actual employment relationship.
Official Name Casa Națională de Pensii Publice (CNPP)
Official English Name National House of Public Pensions
Primary Role National authority administering the public pension and social insurance system funded through statutory contributions withheld from employee pay.
Responsibilities Oversees pension and social insurance benefit administration and coordinates with ANAF on the social contribution components of the consolidated monthly declaration.
Typical Interaction The Employer of Record ensures correct calculation and reporting of pension and health contributions that feed into the CNPP-administered system.
Official Website cnpp.ro
Cross-Border Relevance Directly relevant where an employee is posted to or from Romania and social security coordination under EU rules or bilateral agreements is required.
Official Name Inspectoratul General pentru Imigrări (IGI)
Official English Name General Inspectorate for Immigration
Primary Role National authority responsible for work and residence permits for non-EU/EEA nationals seeking to work in Romania.
Responsibilities Processes work permit applications, verifies employment terms against published requirements and monitors employer compliance with permit conditions.
Typical Interaction Where a non-EU worker is hired through an Employer of Record, IGI reviews the employment offer and terms as part of the work permit process.
Official Website igi.mai.gov.ro
Cross-Border Relevance Essential whenever an Employer of Record supports the employment of a non-EU/EEA national in Romania.
Applicable Legislation

The applicable legislation section identifies the principal rule layers that shape Employer of Record activity in Romania. Employment protection, minimum wage, labour register obligations and social contributions are each governed by distinct instruments that together define the employer's statutory obligations.

Official Title Labour Code (Law no. 53/2003) (Codul Muncii)
Year 2003
Purpose Principal Romanian legislation governing employment relationships, including contract formation, working time, leave, termination grounds and notice periods.
Typical Application Applies to every Employer of Record employment relationship in Romania and governs contract content, probation, suspension and termination procedures.
Related Legislation Implementing government decisions and secondary regulations governing REGES-ONLINE and specific employment procedures.
Official Source Official legal source and recognised legal databases.
Current Status In force, subject to amendment.
Official Title Government Decision on the National Minimum Wage (HG 146/2026)
Year 2026
Purpose Sets the gross national minimum wage guaranteed in payment for full-time employees, raised to RON 4,325 per month from 1 July 2026, up from RON 4,050 in the first half of the year.
Typical Application Used to calculate the statutory pay floor that every Employer of Record salary must meet or exceed, including the hourly equivalent of RON 25.949 per hour from July 2026.
Related Legislation Emergency Ordinance OUG 89/2025 and prior minimum wage decisions establishing the two-step 2026 schedule.
Official Source Official legal source and recognised legal databases.
Current Status In force, subject to annual review.
Official Title Government Decision no. 295/2025 on REGES-ONLINE
Year 2025
Purpose Establishes the REGES-ONLINE general employee register as the mandatory electronic system for recording individual employment agreements, replacing the former REVISAL system.
Typical Application Requires every employer, including an Employer of Record, to register individual employment agreement data no later than one day before the employee's first working day.
Related Legislation Labour Code provisions on contract registration and Labour Inspectorate operating procedures.
Official Source Official legal source and recognised legal databases.
Current Status In force, subject to amendment.
Official Title Fiscal Code and Consolidated Monthly Declaration Framework (Form D112)
Year 2003, as amended
Purpose Governs income tax withholding and the consolidated monthly declaration covering income tax, social contributions and insured persons filed with ANAF.
Typical Application Determines the Employer of Record's monthly filing and payment obligation, due by the 25th of the month following the salary payment month.
Related Legislation Social insurance legislation governing pension, health and work insurance contribution rates.
Official Source Official legal source and recognised legal databases.
Current Status In force, subject to amendment.
Process Flow

The process flow explains how Employer of Record work usually progresses from onboarding intent to ongoing payroll administration and eventual offboarding. It matters because Employer of Record work is a continuous operating relationship, not a single filing event.

1. Client and Role AssessmentConfirm the client's hiring intent, the role, reporting line and whether the arrangement will genuinely function as an employment relationship in Romania.
2. Minimum Wage and Terms MappingConfirm the applicable national minimum wage and align proposed salary, working time and benefit terms accordingly.
3. Contract IssuanceIssue a Romanian employment contract in the Employer of Record's name, specifying role, salary, working time and notice terms in accordance with the Labour Code.
4. REGES-ONLINE and Tax RegistrationRegister the individual employment agreement in REGES-ONLINE no later than one day before the start date and confirm the employer's ANAF tax registration.
5. Monthly Payroll ExecutionCalculate gross pay, withhold income tax and social contributions, and file the consolidated monthly declaration Form D112 with ANAF by the 25th of the following month.
6. Ongoing Compliance AdministrationAdminister leave, sick pay coordination, benefits and any updates to the REGES-ONLINE register as the employment relationship evolves.
7. Offboarding or TransitionProcess termination in line with the Labour Code, update REGES-ONLINE accordingly, or support transfer of the employee to the client's own Romanian entity where one is later established.
Typical OutputsSigned employment contracts, REGES-ONLINE registration confirmations, monthly payslips, Form D112 filings, statutory contribution remittances and termination documentation.
Decision Tree

The decision tree simplifies threshold questions that commonly determine whether an Employer of Record is the correct route in Romania. It is presented as a logical workflow so that the reader can follow the sequence as an operational progression rather than as disconnected legal labels.

  1. Identify whether the business needs an employment relationship or an independent contractor engagement in Romania.
  2. Confirm whether the business already has, or intends to establish, its own Romanian legal entity.
  3. If no local entity exists or is planned in the short term, assess whether an Employer of Record can lawfully support the intended role.
  4. Confirm the applicable national minimum wage and align the proposed compensation package accordingly.
  5. Confirm whether the worker is an EU/EEA national or requires work permit sponsorship through the General Inspectorate for Immigration.
  6. Set up REGES-ONLINE registration, payroll and Form D112 filing processes, then align ongoing administration with actual working arrangements.

For many clients, Romania is one hiring location inside a wider international workforce strategy, which means tax residency, social security coordination and work authorisation often need cross-jurisdiction analysis from the outset.

RecognitionRomanian Employer of Record arrangements often function as one layer within a broader multi-country hiring strategy rather than an isolated domestic payroll exercise.

Language ConsiderationsDomestic administration requires Romanian-language contracts and filings, while client reporting and multinational HR policy are often handled in English.

Practical ConsiderationsCross-border Employer of Record arrangements usually work best when Romanian payroll administration, EU coordination rules and the client's home-country obligations are treated as one coordinated compliance architecture.

FAQ

The FAQ section collects recurring threshold questions in a concise handbook format.

Does Romania Have a Statutory Minimum Wage That an Employer of Record Must Apply?Yes. Romania sets a national gross minimum wage by government decision, currently RON 4,325 per month for standard full-time employment from 1 July 2026, and any Employer of Record salary must meet or exceed this floor.
Who Administers Payroll Tax and Social Contributions for an Employer of Record in Romania?ANAF administers income tax withholding and the consolidated monthly declaration Form D112, while contract registration is filed with the Labour Inspectorate through REGES-ONLINE.
Is Contract Registration in REGES-ONLINE Mandatory for an Employer of Record Operating in Romania?Yes. Every individual employment contract must be registered in the REGES-ONLINE general employee register, generally no later than one day before the employee begins work, replacing the former REVISAL system.
Can a Foreign Company Use an Employer of Record Instead of Establishing a Romanian Entity?Yes. Many foreign companies use an Employer of Record to hire staff in Romania lawfully without first incorporating a local entity, while still meeting Romanian payroll, tax, social contribution and labour law requirements.
Is Payroll Filing Alone Enough for Compliance?No. Correct Employer of Record compliance also requires REGES-ONLINE registration, Labour Code compliance, minimum wage alignment and, where relevant, cross-border social security coordination.
